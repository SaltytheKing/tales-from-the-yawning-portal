# Backstory
Kraum Mirthridge began life abandoned by his parents at the Font of Knowledge, a Temple of Oghma. One of the scholars, Leon Mirthridge, took pity on Kraum and decided to raise the half-orc at the temple teaching him the ways of the world. Kraum not only displayed an aptitude for martial talents typical of most [[Half-Orc]] but he also had an insatiable thirst for knowledge often spending days awake reading every book that peaked his interest within The Great Library. Leon adopted Kraum seeing the potential in the young boy. When Kraum turned eighteen he join the City Guard as he believed it would be the best way to get used to interacting with people after spending his whole life in the library. After six years within the city guard Kraum's thirst for knowledge sparked a feeling of wanderlust within him. Wanting to see other lands and people Kraum decided to leave the city guard and live as a traveler. Before Kraum left Leon gave him one of the most important artifacts of The Great Library, a book that acts as a direct link to the entirety of the Library, wit this artifact one could add any information they obtain to the library by writing in its pages, conversely one could obtain any information from the book by asking questions. After a few years of traveling meeting various people and cultures, seeing wonderous views Kraum hears about the [[Yawning Portal Inn]] and suddenly that old feeling of wanderlust comes back, the idea of seeing new places and beings beyond anyone's imagination brought a smile to Kraum's face. The thought of him not only discovering new places but possibly discovering knowledge that could change our world and maybe he could finally add something to his father's book. Kraum knew that going in solo would be a death sentence so he decided to join and adventuring company that he crossed paths with during his travels.
