# Backstory
Born to [[Human]] father and [[Wood Elf]] mother, Owyn grew up shunned from the people of his village throughout his life. One day, the villagers blamed Owyn for a crime he did not commit, and ran him out to the neighboring forest. There, he honed the druidic teachings of his mother, as well as the martial skill with weapons his father knew. Owyn has learned to respect the residents and aspects of the forest, focusing his magic towards the earth, water, and the animals around him. Due to years living alone, he does not possess very sharp social skills, but is a good person at heart.
