_Level 1 Divination_
**Casting Time:** Action or Ritual
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 10 minutes
You gain the ability to comprehend and verbally communicate with [[Beast]]s for the duration. The knowledge and awareness of many [[Beast]]s is limited by their intelligence, but at minimum, [[Beast]]s can give you information about nearby locations and monsters, including whatever they can perceive or have perceived within the past day. You might be able to persuade a [[Beast]] to perform a small favor for you, at the DM's discretion.
