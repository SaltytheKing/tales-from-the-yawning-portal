**Typical Speakers:** [[Elemental]]s

**Script:** [[Dwarvish]]

[[Primordial]] is a guttural [[Languages]], filled with harsh syllables and hard consonants.

This [Language]] is a family which includes the following dialects: [[Aquan (Primordial)]], [[Auran (Primordial)]], [[Ignan (Primordial)]], [[Terran (Primordial)]]. Creatures that speak different dialects of the same [[Languages]] can communicate with one another.
