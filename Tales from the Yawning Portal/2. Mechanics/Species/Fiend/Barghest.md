Vicious and intelligent canines from the Lower Planes, barghests can be encountered inside the [[Doomvault]]. Unlike other creatures of their ilk, the barghests in [[Dead in Thay]] can be bargained with because they resent their imprisonment, and thus they might become allies (after a fashion) of the [[Player Character]]s.
