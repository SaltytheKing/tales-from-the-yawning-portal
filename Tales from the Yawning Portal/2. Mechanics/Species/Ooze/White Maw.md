In their laboratories inside the [[Doomvault]], the [[Red Wizards]] experiment with a process to create sentient [[Ooze]]s. The creature they call White Maw is one of their earliest achievements--a gray [[Ooze]] of enormous size that has intelligence and can communicate but is prone to violent outbursts.
***Ooze Nature.*** White Maw doesn't require sleep.
