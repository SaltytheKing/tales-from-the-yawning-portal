The [[Immortal Caverns]] inside the [[Doomvault]] ([[Dead in Thay]]) are home to a number of bizarre creatures, of which the Ooze Master is perhaps the strangest. It initially appears in a pillar composed of thick, red, ooze-like stuff. The creature is actually inside the pillar, and when it is roused, it emerges with [[Evil]] intent. 
***Undead Nature.*** The Ooze Master doesn't require air, food, drink, or sleep.

***Huge [[Undead]], [[Lawful]]-[[Evil]]***

**[[Languages]]s** [[Common]], [[Primordial]], [[Thayan (Language)]]
