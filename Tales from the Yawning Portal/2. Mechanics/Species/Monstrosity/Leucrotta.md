The Leucrotta is every bit as horrible as its grotesque appearance would suggest: the body of a [[Hyena]] perched atop the spindly legs of a [[Deer]] and topped with the head of a [[Giant Badger]]. A group of these creatures lurks inside of a location in the [[Doomvault]] ([[Dead in Thay]]), where they use their talent for mimicry to call for help--enticing would-be prey to come to their rescue before discovering the truth of the matter.
