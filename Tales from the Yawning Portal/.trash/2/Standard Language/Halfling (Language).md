_Standard [[Languages]]_ in [[Player's Handbook '14]]

**Typical Speakers:** [[Halfling]]s

**Script:** [[Common]]

The [[Halfling]] [[Languages]] isn't secret, but [[Halfling]]s are loath to share it with others. They write very little, so they don't have a rich body of literature. Their oral tradition, however, is very strong. Almost all [[Halfling]]s speak [[Common]] to converse with the people in whose lands they dwell or through which they are traveling.
