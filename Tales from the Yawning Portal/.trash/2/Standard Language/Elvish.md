**Typical Speakers:** [[Elf]]

**Script:** Elvish

Elvish is fluid, with subtle intonations and intricate grammar. Elven literature is rich and varied, and their songs and poems are famous among other species. Many [[Bard]]s learn their [[Languages]] so they can add Elvish ballads to their repertoires.
