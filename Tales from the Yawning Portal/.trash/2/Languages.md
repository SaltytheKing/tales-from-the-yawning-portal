Your species indicates the languages your character can speak by default, and your [[Background]] might give you access to one or more additional languages of your choice. Note these languages on your character sheet.

Some of these languages are actually families of languages with many dialects. For example, the Primordial language includes the [[Auran (Primordial)]], [[Aquan (Primordial)]], [[Ignan (Primordial)]], and [[Terran (Primordial)]] dialects, one for each of the four elemental planes. Creatures that speak different dialects of the same language can communicate with one another.
