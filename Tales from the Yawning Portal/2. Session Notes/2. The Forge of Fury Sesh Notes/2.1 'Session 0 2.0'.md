## Introduction
*Queue the [[Intro Song]]!*
D&D runs for a few hours, so always be prepared for the inevitable. Both in game and the real world. We're all adults and can stop if needed to accommodate for anyone at any time, but we want to all be ready to play ASAP because time is precious. 
- Please have snacks/drinks ready.
- Let's get smoke and bathroom breaks out of the way as early as possible.
- Have your dice, towers/trays, notes and character sheets out and ready to use when needed.
- We can joke around and have fun out of character, but if you hear [[The Fart Sound]], please reel it in and we can get back to D&D!

Everyone here is really good about roleplaying and keeping things immersed. Remember that just because you as a player know things, it doesn't mean that your character does!
- Keep meta info to a minimum, unless it's truly necessary.
- Don't be rude when someone is immersed in their moment as their character and try your hardest to not interrupt anyone that is roleplaying.
- If you want to roll for something, please wait to be prompted for the roll or ask and wait for the okay to roll- and feel free to ask a second time if I did not hear you the first time!

If at anytime someone feels uncomfortable with anything that has been said, shown, implied, etc. please let me know. Whether it's in a DM on Discord, a whisper on Roll20 or outright saying it aloud for everyone to hear. Your voices are all important to everyone else at the virtual tabletop!
