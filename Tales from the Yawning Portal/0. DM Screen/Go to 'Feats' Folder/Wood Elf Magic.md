*Prerequisite:* [[Wood Elf]]
You learn the magic of the primeval woods, which are revered and protected by your people. You learn one [[Druid]] [[Cantrip]] of your choice. You also learn the [[Longstrider]] and [[Pass Without Trace]] [[Spell|Spells]], each of which you can cast once without expending a spell slot. You regain the ability to cast these two [[Spell|Spells]] in this way when you finish a long rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for all three spells.
