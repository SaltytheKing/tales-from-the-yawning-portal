Imprisoned inside a mist-filled chamber in the [[Tomb of Horrors (Location)]], Siren serves as evidence of [[Acererak]]'s heartless sense of humor. This mysterious [[Fey]] creature yearns to be released, but an enchantment laid on her by the [[Demilich]] prevents her from telling visitors how to accomplish that task. If anyone succeeds in freeing her, she vows to be a lifelong friend.
