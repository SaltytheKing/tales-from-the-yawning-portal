Clinging to the wall of a water-filled chamber inside the Hidden Shrine of[[Tamoachan (Location)]] is Tecuziztecal, also known as Lord of Snails. This giant slug is not only intelligent, it's capable of speech. If faced with enemies that are too much of a threat for it, the slug might offer to provide assistance, or it might submerge itself to avoid further harm.
