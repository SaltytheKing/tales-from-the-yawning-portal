A [[Neutral]] [[Human]] [[Rogue]]. Vanja will be introduced in [[The Forge of Fury]]. 

Currently settled in [[Blasingdell]] as a general [[Merchant]] at the [[Dell's Hideout]]. Vanja has [[Sergeant Grendar Kuln]] as a "business partner." While Vanja scams most passers by, [[Sergeant Grendar Kuln|Sarge]] ensures them that his prices and wares are both fair and necessary for survival- all for a small portion of the profits.

Vanja is friendly with [[Owyn Sylvanranth]] and [[Erky Timbers]]. [[Erky Timbers|Erky]] was going to meet up with Vanja to sell [[Potion of Healing|Potions of Healing]] when [[Erky Timbers]] was taken away to [[The Sunless Citadel (Location)]].

Speaks [[Common]], [[Dwarvish]], [[Orc (Language)]], *TBD*

# Kraum Beef
He had a falling out with [[Kraum Mirthridge]], though Vanja is to blame for it. [[Kraum Mirthridge|Kraum]] needed to get books and supplies for his school's best supplier, Vanja Khilltahrn. When Vanja told [[Kraum Mirthridge|Kraum]] that he could get EXACTLY what he needed, but had to make a quick run to gather the items, [[Kraum Mirthridge|Kraum]] offered to tag along, out of the kindness of his heart. Vanja offers him a discount for his assistance.

On this trip, things go pretty well but [[Kraum Mirthridge]] realized that Vanja had been disappearing here and there sometimes when he gets more of the supplies. Eventually, [[Kraum Mirthridge]] gets clever and keeps his eyes on Vanja so he doesn't lose the [[Merchant]]. Using nothing more than a torch and intimidation, he's sticking up an old librarian for some tomes. [[Kraum Mirthridge]] noticed that the old man has the one specific book that he added to the school's wishlist, a hard to find one at that. When [[Kraum Mirthridge]] saw this, he speaks up and Vanja, scared, accidentally burned the man. It was truly an accident because he only intended to scare the librarian. When the man gets burned, the books catch flame and things go to shit from there. From there, the [[Half-Orc]] ran to help the old man and put out the fire but Vanja slipped away.

Maybe an hour or two pass and [[Kraum Mirthridge]] was walking home, defeated and feeling guilty. Out of the corner of his eye, he see's that Vanja has been mugged and is chained to something or another. Copperless and bloodied, [[Kraum Mirthridge]] once again offers help but the chains won't let up on his left arm. Shortly after arriving, the assailants return to the scene with weapons when they hear the chains moving around from [[Kraum Mirthridge]] trying to help. The decision process becomes more of a necessity for Vanja's survival. Off with the arm and [[Kraum Mirthridge]] carries his ass all the way back, dropping the supplies they gathered to ensure that he's not weighed down and jumped as well. 

Vanja lost supplies, customers and a limb. His hatred for [[Kraum Mirthridge]] might be his own fault, but Vanja thinks that if he hadn't poked his nose into a place it shouldn't have been, he'd still be whole.
