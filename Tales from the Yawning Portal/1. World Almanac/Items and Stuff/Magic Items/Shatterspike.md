One of the #magicitems introduced in [[The Sunless Citadel (Adventure)]]. 

*Weapon (Longsword), uncommon (requires attunement)*
You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls you make with this magical weapon. If it hits an object, the hit is automatically a critical hit, and it can deal bludgeoning or slashing damage to the object (your choice). Further, damage from nonmagical sources can't harm the weapon.

Owned by [[Sir Braford]], spoken of in [[Oakhurst]] via rumors.
