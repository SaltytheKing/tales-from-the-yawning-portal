One of the #magicitems introduced inside of the [[Yawning Portal Inn]].

*Wondrous Item, rare*

Whenever a creature within 30 feet of the jar speaks a profanity or swear word audibly, the lid automatically unseals itself with a disappointed *tsk* sound. If the creature has at least 5g on their person, the gold is instantly teleported into the jar. The lid then reseals itself with a smug click. The jar's magical effects are suppressed while it is completely covered by cloth, leather, or similar materials and it cannot be opened by through other means (Except by [[Durnan]] as he knows its secrets).

If the swearing creature has less than 5gp, the jar captures their expletive as a breath, converting it into gold instead. The creature immediately loses the ability to speak that specific expletive. To regain the ability to speak the word(s), the creature must use an action to hold 10gp while solemnly declaring: 

***"I pledge my gold to mend my ways; return my swears for future days!"***

When this declaration is made, the 10gp teleport into the jar and the creature regains the ability to speak the forbidden word(s).

The jar fcan hold up to 500gp. When this amount is reached, it trembles slightly and produces a random magic item worth 500gp *(DM's discretion)* that can fit within the jar's dimension. Upon creating this item, the jar shatters into harmless fragments that evaporate into glittering motes of light.

The jar has an AC of 10, 15HP, vulnerability to bludgeoning damage, and immunity to poison and psychic damage. As an action, the jar can be shattered on the ground. Each creature within a 10 foot radius around the impact must succeed on a DC17 Wisdom saving throw, taking 1d4 psychic damage for every 50gp inside the jar (rounded down). The gold is then instantly teleported to the [[Fund for Understanding Civility, and Kindness Annual Sensibility Soiree]], AKA [[Durnan's Vault]].
