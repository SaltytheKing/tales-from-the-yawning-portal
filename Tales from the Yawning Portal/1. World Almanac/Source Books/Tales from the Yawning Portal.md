The campaign that sends every player character through various dungeons from [[The Sunless Citadel (Location)]] through the [[Tomb of Horrors (Adventure)]]. Each dungeon will give the players various magical items and allow them to level up.

The Dungeons/Adventures and the character levels they are made for are as follows:
[[The Sunless Citadel (Adventure)]] (Levels 1-3)
[[The Forge of Fury]] (Levels 3-5)
[[The Hidden Shrine of Tamoachan (Adventure)]] (Levels 5-8)
[[White Plume Mountain (Adventure)]] (Levels 8-9)
[[Dead in Thay]] (Levels 9-11)
[[Against the Giants]] (Levels 11-14)
[[Tomb of Horrors (Adventure)]] (Level 14+)
