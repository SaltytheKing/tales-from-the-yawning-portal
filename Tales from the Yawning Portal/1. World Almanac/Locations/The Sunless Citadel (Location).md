Former home to the [[Children of Ashardalon]]. The area where [[Dydd the Wise]] stabbed [[Ashardalon]] in the heart and the body of the [[Dragon]] fell. [[Gulthias]] was also staked through the heart by [[Atlas Dyddson]] here and caused [[The Gulthias Tree]] to grow, making the citadel itself collapse. 

[[Meepo's Misfits]] showed up and helped the [[Frosti Clan]] kick out [[Durbuluk Tribe]].
