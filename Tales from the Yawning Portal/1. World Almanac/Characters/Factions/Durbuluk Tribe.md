A group of [[Goblin (Species)]] that previously occupied [[The Sunless Citadel (Location)]] under the rule of [[Belak the Outcast]]. They served both [[Durnn]] and [[Grenl]] in separate situations. A small number of the Durbuluk's killed [[Meepo McPotamus|Meepo's]] friends and stole [[Calcryx]] from the [[Frosti Clan]]. Some time after that, [[Meepo's Misfits]] arrived and took back the [[White Dragon Wyrmling]].

Shortly after the defeat of [[Belak the Outcast|Belak]], the [[Frosti Clan]] was able to kick out the surviving members of the tribe. Anyone who refused to leave, was eaten by [[Calcryx]]. The survivors are seeking refuge in a currently unknown location. 

Means "Dominator" in [[Goblin (Language)]]
