A [[Quasit]] that once protected the sarcophagus of [[Failed Dragonpriest, Stanley]] behind the [[Dragon Door]] in [[The Sunless Citadel (Location)]]. He was found by [[Cher 'Horror' Witz]] and now pops in and out freely to give her information and history. 

[[Gulthias]] and Jot have an interesting relationship as well. Jot was more of a pet to him, but a pet that could also do extremely useful tasks for him with his special talents (being unseen by anyone he doesn't want to see him).
