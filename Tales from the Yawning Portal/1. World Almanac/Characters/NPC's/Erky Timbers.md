A [[Gnome]] Acolyte that serves as a [[Cleric]]-[[Warlock]] hybrid. Introduced in [[The Sunless Citadel (Adventure)]], currently allied with the [[Meepo's Misfits]] after being rescued in [[The Sunless Citadel (Location)]]. Humorous at all times, Erky is optimistic towards life and has a Life Ensurance Policy with [[Asmodeus]].

Erky is attracted to [[Owyn Sylvanranth]], but is very friendly with [[Kraum Mirthridge]] and [[Jorah Fellwise]]. He also does business with [[Vanja Khilltahrn]]

***Medium [[Humanoid]] ([[Gnome]]), Any [[Alignment]]***

**[[Languages]]s**  [[Common]], [[Draconic]], [[Gnomish]] and [[Goblin (Language)]]

Can cast [[Light]], [[Sacred Flame]], [[Thaumaturgy]], [[Bless]], [[Cure Wounds]] and [[Sanctuary]]. He can also use [[Channel Divinity-Turn Undead]]
