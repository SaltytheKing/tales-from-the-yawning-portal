A [[Human]] [[Druid]] currently residing in [[The Sunless Citadel (Location)]]. He was expelled from the [[Circle of Land]] for his horrific experiments and studies. When Belak discovered [[The Gulthias Tree]], he sent out the [[Durbuluk Tribe]] to spread its fruits and seeds to release [[Twig Blight]]s out into the world.

He was killed by the collective force of the [[Meepo's Misfits]] and [[Sir Braford]]. In his possession, was the [[Gulthias Staff]].
