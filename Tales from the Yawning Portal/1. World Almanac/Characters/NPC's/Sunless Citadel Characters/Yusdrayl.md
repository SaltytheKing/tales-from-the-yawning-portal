The [[Kobold]]s that occupy a portion of [[The Sunless Citadel (Location)]] ([[Frosti Clan]]) are not a combative lot, as long as they are not attacked. That attitude is shared by their leader, Yusdrayl. She is not automatically hostile to strangers who enter her throne room, and in fact is looking for some help from a group of outsiders ([[Meepo's Misfits]]) that can help her contend with the [[Goblin]]s that live nearby ([[Durbuluk Tribe]]). But if she or any other [[Kobold]]s are threatened or set upon, Yusdrayl defends herself and her tribe as fiercely as she can. As leader, she is in charge of [[Meepo McPotamus]] and personally found the [[Dragon]] egg that hatched into [[Calcryx]]. Can act as a [[Merchant]] for the [[Meepo's Misfits]].

Even though she is [[Lawful]]-[[Evil]], she is quite friendly with [[Owyn Sylvanranth]] and is gal pals with [[Cher 'Horror' Witz]]. She gave the key to the [[Dragon Door]] to the party. She also gave [[Cher 'Horror' Witz]] three [[Elixir of Health]] after learning what was beyond the [[Dragon Door]].
