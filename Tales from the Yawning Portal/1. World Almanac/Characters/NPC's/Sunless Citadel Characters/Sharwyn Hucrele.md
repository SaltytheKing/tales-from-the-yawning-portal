A young adventurer ([[Wizard]]) whose group ([[The Missing Party (SC)]]) ran afoul of a band of [[Goblin (Species)|Goblins]], Sharwyn Hucrele now finds herself enslaved by [[Belak the Outcast]] deep inside [[The Sunless Citadel (Location)]], an unwitting supplicant of [[The Gulthias Tree]]. The corrupting influence has turned her skin thick and rough, as though she were affected by a [[Barkskin]] spell, and has turned the normally [[Good]]-aligned Sharwyn into an [[Evil]] adversary. Her mother, [[Kerowyn Hucrele]] from [[Oakhurst]] has sent the [[Meepo's Misfits]] to rescue her and her brother [[Talgen Hucrele]].
