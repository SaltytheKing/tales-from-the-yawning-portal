A [[Hobgoblin]] that took over the [[Durbuluk Tribe]] by force and now works with/for [[Belak the Outcast]]. Durnn usually doesn't need to get his hands dirty as he sends out large groups of [[Goblin (Species)]]s and [[Hobgoblin]]s to do his dirty work for him. His assistant [[Grenl]] is not a fan of Durnn, but his ego refuses to allow him to see it. His "throne" is in a room just north of [[Goblinville]].

Killed by [[Owyn Sylvanranth]] after he called [[Cher 'Horror' Witz]] a bitch.
