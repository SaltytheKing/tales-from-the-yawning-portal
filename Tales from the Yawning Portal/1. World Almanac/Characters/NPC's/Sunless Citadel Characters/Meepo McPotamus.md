A [[Kobold]] introduced in [[The Sunless Citadel (Adventure)]], currently allied with the [[Meepo's Misfits]] and also an honorary member of the [[Frosti Clan]], run by [[Yusdrayl]]. He lives in the [[The Sunless Citadel (Location)]] and was in charge of watching over [[Calcryx]], as one of the Keeper of the Dragons'. Concerned about his status with [[Yusdrayl]], Meepo is always delighted to meet new people. He can also be rather selfish.

Meepo hates everyone from the [[Durbuluk Tribe]] and likely would dislike any [[Goblin (Species)]] after losing [[Calcryx]] and his comrades before meeting the [[Meepo's Misfits]]. He is especially friendly with [[Owyn Sylvanranth]] and [[Kraum Mirthridge]], though he would like to be closer with [[Cher 'Horror' Witz]] to help him better understand [[Common]]! After thinking that the party had made peace with the [[Durbuluk Tribe]], Meepo was officially made a member of the [[Frosti Clan]]. Shortly after that, he was stabbed in the back by [[Grenl]] and killed.

Uses a [[Bag of Holding]] as a means of keeping his "army of [[Calcryx Jr.]]s" and "arsenal" and refuses to use it any other way. When fully stocked from his tribes' farm, he has access to a [[Flask of Acid]], [[Alchemist's Fire]], a [[Basket of Centipedes]], a [[Green Slime Pot]], a [[Rot Grub Pot]], a [[Scorpion on a Stick]], a [[Skunk in a Cage]] and a [[Wasp Nest in a Bag]].
