Known as "Gulthias, the Firstborn" and "Keeper of the heart". He was the leader of the [[Children of Ashardalon]]. Driving himself mad over hundreds of years obsessing over the death of [[Ashardalon]] and refusing to admit that the [[Dragon]] was truly dead, he performed dark rituals to bring [[Ashardalon]] back to this plane of existence. Once Gulthias got the heart to beat, he and the followers at [[The Nightfang Spire]] drank a crimson drink that turned them [[Undead]] and him into a [[Vampire]]. Once this transformation occurred, he returned to [[The Sunless Citadel (Location)]] to retrieve [[Treasures of the Fire Lords]] in hopes to revive [[Ashardalon]]. Little did he know, a [[Atlas Dyddson]] had arrived to [[The Sunless Citadel (Location)]] as well to prevent just that. After an epic battle, the [[Atlas Dyddson]] staked Gulthias' heart, and that stake took root, causing the citadel to sink and turning Gulthias into [[The Gulthias Tree]].
