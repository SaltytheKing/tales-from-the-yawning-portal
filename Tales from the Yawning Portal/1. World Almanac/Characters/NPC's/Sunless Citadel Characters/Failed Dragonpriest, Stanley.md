A higher up member of the [[Children of Ashardalon]] who served in [[The Sunless Citadel (Location)]]. Stanley always wanted to show [[Gulthias]] that he was the best [[Elf]] in the cult. He turned himself into a [[Troll]] using [[True Polymorph]] after hearing that [[Gulthias]] had made himself a [[Vampire]]. After handing [[Gulthias]] the book, [[Treasures of the Fire Lords]], he denied Stanley, saying that he had no need for a monstrous pet. Though, he allowed Stanley to keep his status and had him sequestered.

[[Meepo's Misfits]] ended up killing Stanley and freeing [[Jot]] in the process during [[The Sunless Citadel (Adventure)]].
